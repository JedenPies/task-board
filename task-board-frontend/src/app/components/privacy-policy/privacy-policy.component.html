1. Introduction

Welcome to the Kanban Board Application. User privacy is highly respected, and protecting personal data is a top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when using this service.

2. Information Collected

Data collection is strictly minimized. When logging in using a third-party authentication provider (such as GitHub, Google, Facebook, or LinkedIn), only the following information provided by that service is collected:

  • Your Provider User ID
  • Your Display Name
  • Your Username (if provided)

Note: Passwords for these third-party services are never collected or stored.

3. How Information is Used

The collected information is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain your active session.
  • To associate you with your private and public Kanban boards.
  • To display your name within the application interface.

4. Data Storage and Cookies

Standard web technologies, such as HTTP-only Cookies and Local Storage, are used to securely store authentication tokens (JWT). These are strictly necessary for the application to function and keep you logged in.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Personal information is never sold, rented, or traded to any third parties. Furthermore, user data is not used for advertising purposes.

6. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal data at any time through your Profile Settings.

Right to be forgotten: If you choose to delete your account, a strict anonymization process is triggered. Your identifiable data (Display Name, Username, and Provider ID) is permanently overwritten with random, non-identifiable cryptographic strings. Any public boards you created will remain available to the public, but they will be permanently decoupled from your identity.

7. Contact Me

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out directly or open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
